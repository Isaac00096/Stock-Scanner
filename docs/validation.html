How to read this. Every setup is tested separately in each market Trade Nation offers. Older data is the in-sample test; the most recent 3 years (out-of-sample) is the number that counts. "Chance of a win" is how often past trades made money, with the 95% range in brackets. "Random entries" is the same trade taken on random days; a setup has to beat it. Only validated setups give trade signals in that market; watchlist setups appear marked "paper trade only". Returns are per trade, after that market's cost.

Caveats: only instruments listed today are tested (delisted losers are missing, which flatters results); small markets (indices, commodities, crypto) have few instruments, so most setups there will show "insufficient" until more history builds up. Past results don't guarantee future ones.
