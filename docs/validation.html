How to read this. Every setup is tested separately in each market Trade Nation offers. Older data is the in-sample test; the most recent 3 years (out-of-sample) is the number that counts. Caveats: only instruments listed today are tested (delisted losers are missing, which flatters results); small markets (indices, commodities, crypto) have few instruments, so most setups there will show "insufficient" until more history builds up. Past results don't guarantee future ones.
